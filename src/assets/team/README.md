# Team photos

Put a photo of a team member in this folder and it appears on the Lab and
Supervision pages next time the site is built. Until then their initials
are shown.

Name each file after the person, in lower case with hyphens, as JPG, PNG or
WebP. For example:

- `malik-faizan.jpg`
- `athira-kalavampara-raghunadhan.jpg`
- `nirmal-d-wickramasinghe.png`

A square photo of about 400 × 400 pixels works best. Please use photos that
each person has sent you or agreed to.
